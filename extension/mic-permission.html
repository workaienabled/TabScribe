Include your own voice in saved transcripts

TabScribe transcribes the meeting from the tab. The tab carries every voice except yours, so your own speech is added from your microphone.

  • Your microphone is added to the downloaded file only — never shown in the live transcript.
  • It is never played back through your speakers.
  • You can turn it off any time under the gear in the panel.

Chrome only shows the microphone prompt on a normal page, which is why this tab opened. Allowing here applies to TabScribe everywhere, once.
